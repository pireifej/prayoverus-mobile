---
name: Crisis prayer posting
description: Product rationale for handling self-harm-related prayer requests
---

For self-harm-related prayer requests, show crisis resources prominently before any posting action, but preserve an explicit user choice to post the original wording. Do not present revised prayer wording as the primary crisis response.

**Why:** A person may be asking for urgent prayer and community support; blocking the request entirely could increase isolation. Support should be offered first without removing user autonomy.

**How to apply:** Keep crisis handling separate from ordinary moderation suggestions. Never auto-post, never rewrite the original request, and keep emergency contact actions visible.
