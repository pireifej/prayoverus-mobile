---
name: Notification email change
description: Prayer support emails replaced with nightly summary digest — good feature to highlight in future update broadcasts
---

**What changed:** Users no longer receive one email per prayer (i.e. every time someone prays for your request). That was annoying and spammy. Now:
- Push notification only when someone prays for your request
- One nightly summary email listing everyone who prayed for your requests that day

**Why:** Users complained about email overload. Nightly digest is friendlier.

**How to apply:** When drafting the next user broadcast / update email, include this as a highlighted improvement. Frame it as: "We've cleaned up notifications — instead of one email per prayer, you now get a single nightly summary of everyone who prayed for you."
